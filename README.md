# nextCatchup 🌍

A lightweight timezone converter to help you coordinate with friends across the globe without the mental math.

**[Live Demo](https://wansze.github.io/nextCatchup/)**

---

## The Problem

Friends scattered across timezones mean constantly converting times in your head at midnight. No more.

## What It Does

Pick a date and drag the time slider — three clocks update in real time showing what that moment looks like in **London**, **Kuala Lumpur**, and **Toronto**. Click any clock to make it the controlling timezone, so you can think in your friend's local time instead of doing the math.

## Features

- **Real-time timezone conversion** — Instantly see the same moment across three cities
- **Interactive controls** — Change which timezone is primary with a single click
- **Visual feedback** — Three analog clocks show time differences at a glance
- **Zero dependencies** — Vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks or build steps
